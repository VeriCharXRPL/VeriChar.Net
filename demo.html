7. XRP Ledger (XRPL) layer

New module

VeriChar uses the XRP Ledger (XRPL), a decentralized blockchain, for fast, low-cost settlement and public escrow records.

Low transaction costs

Each transaction costs a small amount of XRP, quoted in drops (1 million drops = 1 XRP). Low fees help make small donations practical.

Fast settlement finality

A new ledger version is created in about 4 to 6 seconds, so emergency orders can reach merchants quickly.

Public provenance

Escrow creation, release and cancellation are recorded on the public ledger, giving a tamper-evident audit trail. Which proof data is recorded on-ledger is still being designed.
