What we are building

VeriChar. Verifiable charity on chain.

Proof before payment. Charity funds are itemized, locked, and released only after proof is verified.

Public accountability. We remove ambiguity from the release of funds and make the flow auditable by design.

How it works

  1. A charity lists a specific need with a price and a named vendor.
  2. Funds are locked and held until proof is submitted.
  3. Proof of invoice and proof of delivery are checked.
  4. Only then are funds released directly to the vendor.

Why XRPL

XRPL provides the public, efficient, auditable settlement layer this system depends on.

It enables fast, low-cost, transparent settlement and proof-based release. For charity, public accountability is not optional. It is the standard.

Transparency

VeriChar is a working prototype. It does not process real donations or Mainnet funds. Backend automated tests have been written and the simulation remains intentionally limited.

Security and Illinois

Not legally impenetrable. No ledger design cancels Illinois law, a contract, or a lawsuit. These concepts are design tools, not legal certainty.

Business model

Free for donors, charities, and vendors. No settlement fees on giving flows. No 1% vendor fee.
