Who we are

VeriChar (Verified Charity On-Chain) is a verification layer for charitable giving, built on the XRP Ledger.

Our mission. Make every charitable dollar traceable, so people can give with confidence and good organizations can prove their impact.

Our vision. A world where “where did my money go?” always has a clear, checkable answer.

“I built VeriChar because real generosity deserves real accountability. Trust should be enforced by code, not demanded by slogans.”

What we do

For donors. Fund one specific, itemized need and watch it move from locked to released.

For charities. Get funded for what you actually need. It’s free, and you never handle the cash.

For vendors. Get paid directly once both proofs are verified.

How it works

  1. A donor funds one specific need.
  2. The funds lock in escrow on the XRP Ledger.
  3. The charity submits an itemized invoice and proof of delivery.
  4. An oracle verifies both proofs.
  5. The vendor is paid directly.

Transparency

VeriChar is a working prototype. It does not process real donations yet. Next comes the XRP Ledger test network with no real funds, then a small pilot after legal and security review.

Cryptographic proof shows a record is authentic and unaltered. It cannot prove the underlying event was honest. VeriChar makes fraud harder and easier to spot, not impossible.

Business model

Free for donors and charities.

Planned revenue: a 1% vendor settlement fee, and licensing audit-ready CSR data to enterprise customers.

How the fee is collected at escrow release is still an open design question.

Get verified

  1. Register your charity name, wallet address and EIN.
  2. Your EIN is checked against IRS non-profit records, off the ledger.
  3. You link your wallet to your website: the account’s Domain field, plus an xrp-ledger.toml file on that domain.

Free for charities. Registration isn’t open yet. Tell us you’re interested.
