Who we are

VeriChar is building verifiable giving on the XRP Ledger — charity people can actually verify.

What we are creating. Itemized needs. Funds locked until invoice and proof of delivery check out. Then payment goes to the vendor, documented on-chain where the design is live.

What we are building

Product. Verifiable giving on XRPL — proof before payment.

For donors. Fund one itemized need and follow the outcome. Free. Live giving is not open yet.

For charities. Verification and itemized needs, free when they open. Clearer records, less admin drag. Not live yet.

For vendors. Named as the payout destination after review. Free to participate. Payouts are not live yet.

For enterprises. SaaS tiers with analytics meant to streamline audits, CSR reporting, and documented giving trails — less overhead, clearer evidence. Preview only today; not certified and not tax or legal advice.

How we intend to achieve it

  1. A donor chooses one specific need.
  2. In a future implementation, the donor could fund an XRPL escrow.
  3. The charity could submit an itemized invoice and delivery evidence for review.
  4. A reviewer or future verification service would assess the evidence.
  5. If approved under the final rules, the escrow could be released to the vendor.

Intended method: lock → prove → pay the vendor. Not a live service yet. The simulation makes no XRPL transactions and verifies no evidence.

Transparency

VeriChar is a working prototype. It does not process real donations or Mainnet funds. Backend automated tests have passed on the XRP Ledger Testnet (conditional escrow create, finish, and cancel; reserve reads), via continuous integration. The public site and donation demo remain simulations. A small pilot would follow legal and security review.

Cryptographic proof shows a record is authentic and unaltered. It cannot prove the underlying event was honest. VeriChar makes fraud harder and easier to spot, not impossible.

Business model

Free for donors, charities, and vendors. No settlement fees on giving flows. No 1% vendor fee.

Path A — Donate to VeriChar. Support the platform. As the system matures, platform funding should be as inspectable as charity funding.

Path B — Enterprise SaaS tiers. Organizations license tools so charitable giving is easier to track, document, and review:

  • Analytics that streamline audits and impact reporting
  • On-chain style trails of gifts when settlement is live — clearer documentation for internal review and external questions
  • Less stress and overhead assembling records; more energy on actual giving
  • Exportable records that can support tax and compliance workflows (we do not file returns for you; not legal or tax advice)

When the rails are live, the goal is simple: more of each gift reaches the need, with proof that is faster and easier to show.
